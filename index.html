Questions Students Ask

Where does my money go?

Public donations fund clean water projects with local partners. Operating costs are covered by a separate group of private donors so 100% of your gift can support water programs.

Can my club or fraternity/sorority fundraise together?

Absolutely. Create a fundraiser for your organization and invite members to share. Friendly campus competitions work great!

Do I get updates on the project I funded?

Yes—projects include reporting and updates so you can see the impact you helped create.
